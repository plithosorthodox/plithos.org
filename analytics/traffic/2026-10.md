# Plithos traffic - October 2026

Collected 13 November 2026 from Cloudflare Web Analytics for the completed UTC
month. Known bots and the preview host were excluded. Counts are estimates
after adaptive sampling at an average interval of ten.

## Summary

- About 490 pageviews and 140 visits.
- Mobile produced 330 pageviews, 67 percent of the total. Desktop produced
  160.
- The calendar home page, Prayers and Library produced 380 pageviews, 78
  percent of the total.
- The United States produced 350 pageviews, 71 percent. The United Kingdom was
  next at 50.
- Explicit locale paths recorded at least 30 Spanish pageviews, 10 Greek and
  10 German. This is a lower bound, not a ranking of all language use.
- Direct or unavailable referrers accounted for 130 estimated visits. External
  referrer hosts were below the public threshold of 20 pageviews.

The field measurements are strong. The larger usability risks are information
architecture, first-screen visibility, tap target size and the amount of work
required to reach specific content, not network rendering speed.

## Product implications

1. Design mobile-first. About two of every three pageviews were mobile.
2. Improve Prayers and Library first. Together they produced 47 percent of all
   pageviews and both currently make readers work through several controls
   before reaching content.
3. Treat Saints discovery as an interface question. Its low share should not
   be read as low interest while the page remains extremely long and hard to
   orient within.
4. Preserve locale measurement before deciding which translation to expand.
   Locale paths identify some usage, but do not capture every in-page language
   change.

The headline 95 percent confidence ranges were 359.84 to 620.16 pageviews and
70.43 to 209.57 visits. Cloudflare marked both ranges invalid because the
sample sizes were too small, so they are recorded for transparency and should
not be used as firm bounds.
