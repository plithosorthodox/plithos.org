On measure. These are set out in full because they are the Church's inheritance. Setting them out in full is not the same as saying anyone must keep all of it. The rule you keep is set by your spiritual father, according to your knowledge, your capability and your circumstances - and the earliest Christian manual we possess says plainly: if you are able to bear all the yoke of the Lord, you will be perfect; but if you are not able, what you are able that do (The Didache 6).
